# Provisional Signal and Routing Schema

The six labels below are **provisional routing questions** inherited from earlier design discussion. They are not validated psychological classes, mutually exclusive categories, or permanent properties of people. They may be renamed, merged, split, or removed after testing.

| Label | Working question | Possible evidence to inspect |
|---|---|---|
| **GAP** | What information, explanation, or capability appears to be missing? | Repeated unanswered questions, clarification requests, conflicting explanations |
| **FOLLOW** | What are people tracking, imitating, anticipating, or following? | Repeated references, updates, participation sequences, topic migration |
| **VERIFY** | Which claim, event, or explanation is being questioned? | Requests for proof, conflicting accounts, corrections, primary records |
| **STAKE** | What interests, risks, norms, or consequences appear to be at stake? | Explicit impacts, trade-offs, responsibility claims, affected-party accounts |
| **ACTION** | What action is requested, attempted, offered, or reported? | Explicit calls to action, instructions sought, documented actions and outcomes |
| **EXPRESS** | What feelings, identity claims, or social meanings are being expressed? | First-person statements, expressive forms, symbolic language |

## Use as routing, not classification

A topic window may contain several route candidates. Do not force one label if the evidence supports several, and do not force any label if evidence is insufficient.

For each route candidate, retain its trigger observation, source and timestamp, next question, counterevidence, and status (`HYPOTHESIS`, `SUPPORTED`, `CONTESTED`, `UNKNOWN`, `EXPIRED`).

A high-volume signal is not necessarily representative, true, or important. Repetition is not independent corroboration.
