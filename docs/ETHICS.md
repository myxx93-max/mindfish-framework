# Ethics, Privacy, and Use Boundaries

## Topic-level, not person-level

Mindfish describes provisional patterns around topics and time windows. It must not assign enduring psychological types, infer sensitive traits, deanonymize participants, or construct private dossiers.

## Data boundaries

- Use public, lawfully accessible, or explicitly authorized sources.
- Respect platform access controls, terms, and community expectations.
- Do not publish private messages, personal identifiers, credentials, or non-consensual personal data.
- Minimize retained data; prefer source references and aggregate descriptions.
- Preserve provenance and context.

## Anti-manipulation boundary

Do not use the framework to exploit inferred vulnerabilities, intensify distress, manipulate political or religious beliefs, or target people based on sensitive characteristics.

A route such as `STAKE` or `EXPRESS` is a prompt for careful observation, not a license to exploit emotion.

High volume does not mean representativeness. Silence does not mean agreement. Repetition does not mean independent confirmation. UNKNOWN and contested interpretations must remain visible.

Mindfish output does not authorize publication, outreach, moderation, account operations, financial decisions, or other consequential actions.
