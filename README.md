# Mindfish Cognitive Framework

**A dynamic framework for deciding where a hotspot deserves further observation, evidence, verification, or action.**

**心智鱼：不是给人贴标签，而是判断一个热点当前值得朝哪个方向观察、补充、验证或行动。**

> A living hypothesis about a changing cognitive state — not a fixed profile of a person.

## What Mindfish is

Mindfish is a user-defined abstract tool for hotspot-oriented retrieval and content direction. A “fish” is a provisional hypothesis about a changing cognitive state around a topic, event, or unresolved question.

It is **not** a literal biological model, personality type, stable user classification, universal psychological taxonomy, or validated prediction engine. It does not claim to reveal what every individual privately thinks.

Its practical question is: **Given what is happening around a hotspot now, what should we observe, supplement, verify, or do next?**

## The original metaphor

| Metaphor | Working meaning |
|---|---|
| Water | The real-world information environment |
| Flow | Attention migration and collective tension |
| Fish | A provisional, time-bounded cognitive-state hypothesis |
| Hook | An unresolved contradiction, question, expectation, or tension |
| Float | Observable traces such as comments, dwell, retelling, self-disclosure, or disagreement |
| Net | The evidence environment: Reality Tape, comments, hot topics, motifs, counterexamples, and feedback |

These are conceptual mappings, not proof that every signal is observable or causally connected.

## Operating loop

1. Detect a hotspot or meaningful change.
2. Form small, explicit Mindfish hypotheses.
3. Attach source provenance, time window, and uncertainty.
4. Choose the next direction: **observe**, **supplement**, **verify**, or **act**.
5. Seek counterevidence, not only confirming examples.
6. Keep, split, merge, downgrade, reject, or expire the hypothesis.
7. Record outcomes without treating correlation as causation.

## Relationship to Attention Flow

Attention Flow may provide a temporal and prioritization background: where attention appears to move and which topics may deserve observation. It does not establish factual truth, explain why attention moved, replace counterevidence, or grant safety or publication authorization.

An earlier Attention Flow M1 result was invalidated because of target-geometry problems. Later clean-target work must be evaluated independently. This repository does not claim that Attention Flow or Mindfish is production-validated.

## Research status

**Version:** 0.1 — conceptual research prototype  
**Implementation:** exploratory; no production integration is claimed  
**Evidence:** hypotheses and design notes remain separate from independently reproduced findings  
**Data:** no private datasets, account data, credentials, or unpublished production code are included

Earlier local Mindfish/hot-router work described its model as a **starting hypothesis**, not business truth. Reproducibility within a local experiment does not establish predictive accuracy, causal validity, or production usefulness.

## Invitation

We welcome criticism from information science, cognitive science, sociology, HCI, journalism, AI-agent engineering, and adjacent fields.

A counterexample showing that a proposed “fish” is actually several different things — or that the concept adds no value over a simpler method — is a valuable contribution.

**Mindfish should become clearer through evidence and disagreement, not through accumulating labels.**
