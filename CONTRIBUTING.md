# Contributing

Mindfish is an open research prototype. Critical review and counterexamples are especially valuable.

## Useful contributions
- clarify the concept or identify ambiguity;
- challenge a signal or lifecycle label;
- submit a counterexample;
- propose a simpler competing model;
- review privacy, representation, and evidence boundaries;
- contribute a reproducible evaluation using shareable, authorized data.

## Evidence requirements

For empirical claims, include the exact question, source and data provenance, observation window, sampling method, inclusion/exclusion rules, reproduction procedure, limitations, and counterexamples.

Do not label synthetic or illustrative examples as real-world evidence. Mark hypothetical examples explicitly.

## Language and rights

Contributions may be in English or Chinese. Preserve original terms and explain translation choices. Do not submit personal data, private conversations, credentials, leaked datasets, or material without appropriate rights.

Disagreement is welcome. A contribution that demonstrates a category is unnecessary or a hypothesis is false is valuable.
